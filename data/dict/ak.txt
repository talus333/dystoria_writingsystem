ake		See Ache — a name given to several species of plants; as, smallage, wild celery, parsley.	
akene		Same as Achene.	
aketon		See Acton — a stuffed jacket worn under the mail, or (later) a jacket plated with mail.	
akimbo		With a crook or bend; with the hand on the hip and elbow turned outward.	Middle English in kene bowe.
akin		Of the same kin; related by blood; -- used of persons; as, the two families are near akin.	
akinesia		Paralysis of the motor nerves; loss of movement.	
akinesic		Pertaining to akinesia.	
aknee		On the knee.	
aknow	Obsolete	Earlier form of Acknow.
