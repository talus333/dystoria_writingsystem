cnemial		Pertaining to the shin bone.	
cnida		One of the peculiar stinging, cells found in Coelenterata; a nematocyst; a lasso cell.	
cnidaria		A comprehensive group equivalent to the true Coelenterata, i.e., exclusive of the sponges.	
cnidoblast		One of the cells which, in the Coelenterata, develop into cnidæ.	
cnidocil		The fine filiform process of a cnidoblast.
