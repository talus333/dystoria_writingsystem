ah		An exclamation, expressive of surprise, pity, complaint, entreaty, contempt, threatening, delight, triumph, etc., according to the manner of utterance.	
aha		An exclamation expressing, by different intonations, triumph, mixed with derision or irony, or simple surprise.	
ahead		In or to the front; in advance; onward.	
aheap		In a heap; huddled together.	
aheight	Obsolete	Aloft; on high.	
ahem		An exclamation to call one's attention; hem.	
ahey		Hey; ho.	
ahigh	Obsolete	On high.	
ahold	Obsolete	Near the wind; as, to lay a ship ahold.	
ahorseback		On horseback.	
ahoy		A term used in hailing; as,	
ahriman		The Evil Principle or Being of the ancient Persians; the Prince of Darkness as opposer to Ormuzd, the King of Light.	
ahu		The Asiatic gazelle.	Rapanui ahu.
ahull		With the sails furled, and the helm lashed alee; -- applied to ships in a storm.	
ahungered		Pinched with hunger; very hungry.	
ahura-mazda		The supreme deity, the principle of good, creator of the world, and guardian of mankind.
