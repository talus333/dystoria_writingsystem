tschakmeck		The chameck.	
tschego		A West African anthropoid ape allied to the gorilla and chimpanzee, and by some considered only a variety of the chimpanzee.	
tsebe		The springbok.	
tsetse		A venomous two-winged African fly (Glossina morsitans) whose bite is very poisonous, and even fatal, to horses and cattle, but harmless to men.	Tswana tsetse.
