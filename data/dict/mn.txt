mnemonic		Assisting in memory.	Latin mnemonicus, from Greek μνημονικός.
mnemonical		Assisting in memory.	
mnemonician		One who instructs in the art of improving or using the memory.	
mnemonics		The art of memory; a system of precepts and rules intended to assist the memory; artificial memory.	Greek μνημονικά.
mnemosyne		The goddess of memory and the mother of the Muses.	
mnemotechny		Mnemonics.
