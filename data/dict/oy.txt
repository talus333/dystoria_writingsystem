oyer		A hearing or an inspection, as of a deed, bond, etc., as when a defendant in court prays oyer of a writing.
oyez		Hear; attend; -- a term used by criers of courts to secure silence before making a proclamation.
oylet		See Eyelet — a small hole or perforation to receive a cord or fastener, as in garments, sails, etc.
oyster		(Zoöl.) Any marine bivalve mollusk of the genus Ostrea.
oyster-green		A green membranous seaweed (Ulva) often found growing on oysters but common on stones, piles, etc.
oystering		Gathering, or dredging for, oysters.
oysterling		A young oyster.
