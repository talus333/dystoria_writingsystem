eocene		Pertaining to the first in time of the three subdivisions into which the Tertiary formation is divided by geologists, and alluding to the approximation in its life to that of the present era; as, Eocene deposits. -- n.
eolian		(Geol.) Formed, or deposited, by the action of wind, as dunes.
eolic		See Æolic.
eolipile		Same as Æolipile.
eolis		A genus of nudibranch mollusks having clusters of branchial papillæ along the back.
eon		An immeasurable or infinite space of time; eternity; a long space of time; an age.
eophyte		A fossil plant which is found in the lowest beds of the Silurian age.
eophytic		Of or pertaining to eophytes.
eos		Aurora, the goddess of morn.
eosaurus		An extinct marine reptile from the coal measures of Nova Scotia; -- so named because supposed to be of the earliest known reptiles.
eosin		A yellow or brownish red dyestuff obtained by the action of bromine on fluoresceïn, and named from the fine rose-red which it imparts to silk.
eosphorite		A hydrous phosphate of alumina and manganese.
eozoic		Of or pertaining to rocks or strata older than the Paleozoic, in many of which the eozoön has been found.
eozooen		A peculiar structure found in the Archæan limestones of Canada and other regions.
eozooenal		Pertaining to the eozoön; containing eozoöns; as, eozoönal limestone.
eozoon		A peculiar structure found in the Archæan limestones of Canada and other regions.
eozoonal		Pertaining to the eozoön; containing eozoöns; as, eozoönal limestone.
