ivied		Overgrown with ivy.	
ivoride		A composition resembling ivory in appearance and used as a substitute for it.	
ivory		The hard, white, opaque, fine-grained substance constituting the tusks of the elephant.	Middle English ivorie, from Anglo-Norman ivurie.
ivory-bill		A large, handsome, North American woodpecker (Campephilus principalis), having a large, sharp, ivory-colored beak.	
ivorytype		A picture produced by superposing a very light print, rendered translucent by varnish, and tinted upon the back, upon a stronger print, so as to give the effect of a photograph in natural colors; -- called also hellenotype.	
ivy	Obsolete	A plant of the genus Hedera (H. helix), common in Europe.	Middle English ivi, from Old English ifig.
ivy-mantled		Covered with ivy.
