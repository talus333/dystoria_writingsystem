ryder		Etym: A gold coin of Zealand equal to 14 florins, about $ 5.60.	
rye		(Bot.) A grain yielded by a hardy cereal grass (Secale cereale), closely allied to wheat; also, the plant itself.	Old English ryge.
rynd		A piece of iron crossing the hole in the upper millstone by which the stone is supported on the spindle.	
ryot		A peasant or cultivator of the soil.	
rypophagous		Eating, or subsisting on, filth.	
rys	Obsolete	A branch.	
rysh	Obsolete	Rush, a plant.	
rysimeter		See Rhysimeter — an instrument, acting on the principle of Pitot's tube, for measuring the velocity of a fluid current, the speed of a ship, etc.
