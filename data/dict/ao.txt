aonian		Pertaining to Aonia, Boeotia, or to the Muses, who were supposed to dwell there.
aorist		A tense in the Greek language, which expresses an action as completed in past time, but leaves it, in other respects, wholly indeterminate.
aoristic		Indefinite; pertaining to the aorist tense.
aorta		The great artery which carries the blood from the heart to all parts of the body except the lungs; the main trunk of the arterial system.
aortic		Of or pertaining to the aorta.
aortitis		Inflammation of the aorta.
aoudad		An African sheeplike quadruped (the Ammotragus tragelaphus) having a long mane on the breast and fore legs.
