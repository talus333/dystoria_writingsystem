ewe		The female of the sheep, and of sheeplike animals.
ewe-necked		Having a neck like a ewe; -- said of horses in which the arch of the neck is deficent, being somewhat hollowed out.
ewer		A kind of widemouthed pitcher or jug; esp., one used to hold water for the toilet.
ewery		An office or place of household service where the ewers were formerly kept.
