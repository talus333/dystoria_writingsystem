xiphias		(Zoöl.) A genus of fishes comprising the common swordfish.
xiphidium		A genus of plants of the order Hæmodraceæ, having two-ranked, sword-shaped leaves.
xiphioid		Of, pertaining to, or resembling, a cetacean of the genus Xiphius or family Xiphiidæ.
xiphiplastron		The posterior, or fourth, lateral plate in the plastron of turtles; -- called also xiphisternum.
xiphisternum		(a) The posterior segment, or extremity, of the sternum; -- sometimes called metasternum, ensiform cartilage, ensiform process, or xiphoid process.
xiphius		A genus of cetaceans having a long, pointed, bony beak, usually two tusklike teeth in the lower jaw, but no teeth in the upper jaw.
xiphodon		An extinct genus of artiodactylous mammals found in the European Tertiary formations.
xiphoid		(a) Like a sword; ensiform. (b) Of or pertaining to the xiphoid process; xiphoidian.
xiphoidian		Xiphoid.
xiphophyllous		Having sword-shaped leaves.
xiphosura		See Xiphura — same as Limuloidea.
xiphura		Same as Limuloidea.
