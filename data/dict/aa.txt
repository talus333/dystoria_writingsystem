aam		A Dutch and German measure of liquids, varying in different cities, being at Amsterdam about 41 wine gallons, at Antwerp 36½, at Hamburg 38¼.	
aard-vark		An edentate mammal, of the genus Orycteropus, somewhat resembling a pig, common in some parts of Southern Africa.	
aard-wolf		A carnivorous quadruped (Proteles Lalandii), of South Africa, resembling the fox and hyena.	
aaronic		Pertaining to Aaron, the first high priest of the Jews.	
aaronical		Pertaining to Aaron, the first high priest of the Jews.
