udal		In Shetland and Orkney, a freehold; property held by udal, or allodial, right.
udalborn		Vars. of Odal, etc. Obs. exc. in Shetland and the Orkney Islands, where udal designates land held in fee simple without any charter and free of any feudal character.
udaler		Vars. of Odal, etc. Obs. exc. in Shetland and the Orkney Islands, where udal designates land held in fee simple without any charter and free of any feudal character.
udalman		Vars. of Odal, etc. Obs. exc. in Shetland and the Orkney Islands, where udal designates land held in fee simple without any charter and free of any feudal character.
udder		(Anat.) The glandular organ in which milk is secreted and stored; -- popularly called the bag in cows and other quadrupeds.
uddered		Having an udder or udders.
udderless	Poetic	Hence, without mother's milk; motherless; as, udderless lambs.
udometer		A rain gauge.
